Spotting a Neutralization

What makes it a neutralization? Statement B doesn't engage with whether the action was right or wrong — it argues that because nothing visibly went wrong, there's nothing to address. That's the signature move of a neutralization technique: it sidesteps the ethical question rather than answering it. The field guide notes that these techniques are dangerous precisely because they feel reasonable at first glance.

How to Resist

1Name it. Say out loud: "I'm about to use a neutralization technique."
2Ask: "Would I be comfortable if this reasoning appeared on the front page?"
3Ask: "Am I answering the ethical question — or avoiding it?"
4Run the dilemma through the five frameworks in Activity 1.
