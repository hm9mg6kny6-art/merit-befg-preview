Teaching · What Is a Framework?

Framework or Gut Reaction?

One of these statements reflects an ethical framework — a reasoned principle that could apply consistently across situations. Tap the one you think it is.

Why frameworks matter. Gut reactions are real — but they can mislead, especially under pressure. Precedent-based reasoning ("we've done it before") can lock in bad norms. An ethical framework gives you a principled basis that holds up under scrutiny, can be explained to others, and can be applied consistently — not just when it's convenient.

Five Ethical Perspectives

FrameworkCore QuestionKey Tool
DeontologyWhat duties or rules bind me here, regardless of outcome?Universalizability · Rights · Golden Rule · Justice
ConsequentialismWhat action produces the greatest benefit for everyone affected?Identify all stakeholders · Weigh outcomes · Act utilitarianism vs. rule utilitarianism
Virtue EthicsWhat would a person of good character do?Mirror test · Pillow test · Disclosure test
Social ContractsWhat rule could all parties — including those most affected — agree to?Rawls' veil of ignorance · Fairness of process and outcome
Ethics of CareWhat do my specific relationships require of me here?Identify closest obligations · Balance relationships · Watch for proximity bias

The Eight-Step Decision Process

1Identify the important facts.
2Identify the ethical issue(s) and the values in tension.
3Identify the stakeholders — obvious and non-obvious.
4Identify potential solutions, including creative combinations.
5Assess the solutions using the five ethical perspectives.
6Assess any new solutions that emerged from step 5.
7Act in accordance with your ethical analysis.
8Assess your actions and the outcomes. What did you learn?

Questions to Ask Yourself Every Time

  • Am I protecting others' rights and dignity?
  • Would I be willing to be the other party in this transaction?
  • Who benefits and who is harmed by my decision?
  • What kind of person would this action make me?
  • How would I feel if my reasoning appeared on the news?
  • Am I fulfilling my obligations to those closest to me and most vulnerable?
